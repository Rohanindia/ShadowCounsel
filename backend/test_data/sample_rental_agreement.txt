RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement ("Agreement") is made and executed on this 15th day of March, 2025 at Bangalore, Karnataka, India.

BETWEEN

Mr. Ramesh Kumar Sharma, S/o Shri Mahesh Kumar Sharma, aged about 55 years, residing at No. 42, 3rd Cross, Jayanagar 4th Block, Bangalore - 560041, Karnataka (hereinafter referred to as the "LANDLORD" or "LESSOR", which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context or meaning thereof, include his heirs, successors, legal representatives and assigns) of the FIRST PART;

AND

Ms. Priya Agarwal, D/o Shri Vikram Agarwal, aged about 28 years, working as Software Engineer at TechCorp India Pvt. Ltd., residing at the premises described herein (hereinafter referred to as the "TENANT" or "LESSEE", which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context or meaning thereof, include her heirs, successors, legal representatives and assigns) of the SECOND PART.

The Landlord and Tenant are hereinafter collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

WHEREAS the Landlord is the absolute owner of the residential property bearing No. 15, 2nd Floor, "Sukh Sagar Apartments", 12th Main Road, HSR Layout, Sector 2, Bangalore - 560102, Karnataka, comprising of 2 BHK (two bedrooms, one hall, and one kitchen) with a total carpet area of approximately 1,100 square feet, along with one covered car parking space (hereinafter referred to as the "Premises" or "Demised Premises").

AND WHEREAS the Tenant has approached the Landlord and has requested the Landlord to let out the aforesaid Premises to the Tenant on a rental basis, and the Landlord has agreed to let out the same on the terms and conditions hereinafter set forth.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:

---

CLAUSE 1: TERM AND DURATION

1.1 This Agreement shall commence on 1st April, 2025 (the "Commencement Date") and shall be valid for a period of Eleven (11) months from the Commencement Date, expiring on 28th February, 2026 (the "Expiry Date"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement.

1.2 This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of eleven (11) months each, on the same terms and conditions (subject to the rent escalation clause herein), unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least three (3) months prior to the expiry of the then-current term.

---

CLAUSE 2: MONTHLY RENT AND PAYMENT

2.1 The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 28,000/- (Rupees Twenty-Eight Thousand Only) to the Landlord, payable on or before the 5th day of each calendar month, in advance, through bank transfer (NEFT/UPI) to the Landlord's designated bank account.

2.2 In the event of delay in payment of rent beyond the 5th of any month, the Tenant shall be liable to pay a late fee of Rs. 200/- (Rupees Two Hundred Only) per day of delay, subject to a maximum penalty of Rs. 6,000/- (Rupees Six Thousand Only) per month.

2.3 If the rent remains unpaid for a continuous period exceeding 15 (fifteen) days beyond the due date, the Landlord shall have the right to disconnect water and electricity supply to the Premises, and after 30 (thirty) days of non-payment, the Landlord may initiate eviction proceedings without further notice.

---

CLAUSE 3: SECURITY DEPOSIT

3.1 The Tenant has paid a sum of Rs. 2,80,000/- (Rupees Two Lakhs Eighty Thousand Only), equivalent to ten (10) months' rent, as a refundable Security Deposit to the Landlord at the time of execution of this Agreement, the receipt of which the Landlord hereby acknowledges.

3.2 The Security Deposit shall be refunded to the Tenant within ninety (90) days from the date of vacating the Premises, after deducting any outstanding rent, unpaid utility bills, and the cost of repair for damages caused to the Premises beyond normal wear and tear, as determined solely by the Landlord.

3.3 The Security Deposit shall not carry any interest for the period it remains with the Landlord. The Tenant shall not be entitled to adjust the Security Deposit against the rent for any month(s) during the tenancy.

3.4 In the event the Tenant vacates the Premises before the expiry of the Agreement term without the prior written consent of the Landlord, the Landlord shall be entitled to forfeit 50% of the Security Deposit as liquidated damages, in addition to any other claims.

---

CLAUSE 4: RENT ESCALATION

4.1 The monthly rent shall be increased by 10% (ten percent) at the beginning of each renewal term. The Tenant agrees that this escalation is fair and reasonable, and shall not be subject to negotiation or dispute.

---

CLAUSE 5: MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

5.1 The Tenant shall be responsible for all minor repairs and maintenance of the Premises during the tenancy, including but not limited to plumbing, electrical fittings, painting, pest control, and general upkeep, at the Tenant's own cost and expense.

5.2 Any major structural repairs required to the Premises, including repairs to the roof, foundation, and main walls, shall be the responsibility of the Landlord, provided the Tenant gives prior written notice of such requirement. The Landlord shall complete such repairs within a reasonable time, not exceeding 60 (sixty) days from the date of notice.

5.3 The Tenant shall not make any structural alterations, additions, or improvements to the Premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord. Any alterations made with the Landlord's consent shall become the property of the Landlord upon termination of this Agreement, and the Tenant shall not be entitled to any compensation therefor.

---

CLAUSE 6: USE OF PREMISES

6.1 The Premises shall be used exclusively for residential purposes by the Tenant and her immediate family members only. The Tenant shall not use the Premises or any part thereof for any commercial, illegal, or immoral purpose.

6.2 The Tenant shall not sublet, assign, or transfer this Agreement or the Premises, or any part thereof, to any third party, whether for consideration or otherwise, without the prior written consent of the Landlord. Any such unauthorized subletting shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement and shall entitle the Landlord to immediate termination and eviction.

---

CLAUSE 7: TERMINATION AND NOTICE

7.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by providing written notice of not less than three (3) months to the other Party. During the notice period, the Tenant shall continue to pay rent and maintain the Premises in good condition.

7.2 Notwithstanding Clause 7.1, the Landlord reserves the right to terminate this Agreement with immediate effect and without any notice if:
   (a) The Tenant defaults in payment of rent for two consecutive months;
   (b) The Tenant breaches any material term of this Agreement;
   (c) The Tenant uses the Premises for any purpose other than residential;
   (d) The Tenant causes nuisance or disturbance to neighboring residents;
   (e) The Landlord requires the Premises for his own bona fide personal use.

7.3 In the event of termination under Clause 7.2(e), the Landlord shall provide a minimum of 30 (thirty) days' notice and shall refund the full Security Deposit to the Tenant.

7.4 Upon termination, the Tenant shall vacate the Premises and hand over peaceful possession to the Landlord in the same condition as at the commencement of the tenancy, subject to normal wear and tear. If the Tenant fails to vacate within 15 (fifteen) days of the termination date, the Tenant shall pay double the monthly rent as mesne profits for each month or part thereof of continued occupation.

---

CLAUSE 8: UTILITIES AND SOCIETY CHARGES

8.1 The Tenant shall bear and pay all charges for electricity, water, gas, internet, and any other utilities consumed at the Premises. All utility connections shall remain in the name of the Landlord.

8.2 The Tenant shall pay the monthly society/apartment maintenance charges, currently Rs. 4,500/- (Rupees Four Thousand Five Hundred Only), directly to the apartment owners' association. Any increase in society charges during the tenancy shall be borne entirely by the Tenant.

---

CLAUSE 9: INSPECTION AND ACCESS

9.1 The Landlord or his authorized representative shall have the right to enter and inspect the Premises at any time, with or without prior notice to the Tenant, to ascertain the condition of the Premises and to ensure compliance with the terms of this Agreement.

9.2 The Tenant shall permit prospective tenants or buyers to view the Premises during the last two months of the tenancy, at reasonable hours.

---

CLAUSE 10: INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 The Tenant shall indemnify and hold harmless the Landlord from and against all claims, losses, damages, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including legal fees) arising out of or in connection with:
   (a) Any breach by the Tenant of any term of this Agreement;
   (b) Any injury, death, or property damage occurring in or about the Premises during the tenancy;
   (c) Any act, omission, or negligence of the Tenant, her family members, guests, or visitors;
   (d) Any claim by any third party arising out of the Tenant's use or occupation of the Premises.

10.2 This indemnification obligation shall survive the termination or expiry of this Agreement.

---

CLAUSE 11: INSURANCE

11.1 The Tenant shall, at her own cost, obtain and maintain a comprehensive renter's insurance policy covering her personal belongings, furniture, and fixtures within the Premises against fire, theft, and natural calamities, for the entire duration of the tenancy.

11.2 The Landlord shall not be liable for any loss or damage to the Tenant's property within the Premises, howsoever caused.

---

CLAUSE 12: NON-COMPETE AND RESTRICTION

12.1 During the term of this Agreement and for a period of six (6) months after its termination, the Tenant agrees not to lease or rent any residential property within a radius of 2 (two) kilometers from the Premises from any landlord who is a competitor or known associate of the Landlord in the real estate business.

---

CLAUSE 13: DATA AND PRIVACY

13.1 The Tenant consents to the Landlord collecting, storing, and processing the Tenant's personal information, including but not limited to Aadhaar number, PAN number, employment details, salary slips, and bank statements, for the purposes of this Agreement and for the Landlord's records. The Landlord may share such information with government authorities, financial institutions, or any other third parties as deemed necessary by the Landlord, without requiring further consent from the Tenant.

---

CLAUSE 14: DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND JURISDICTION

14.1 Any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall first be attempted to be resolved through mutual negotiation between the Parties.

14.2 If the dispute is not resolved through negotiation within 30 (thirty) days, either Party may refer the dispute to a sole arbitrator appointed by the Landlord. The arbitration shall be conducted in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, and shall be held at Bangalore, Karnataka. The language of arbitration shall be English.

14.3 The courts of Bangalore, Karnataka shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

---

CLAUSE 15: FORCE MAJEURE

15.1 Neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performing its obligations under this Agreement if such failure or delay results from circumstances beyond the reasonable control of that Party, including but not limited to natural disasters, epidemics, government orders, war, civil unrest, or any act of God.

---

CLAUSE 16: MISCELLANEOUS

16.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, warranties, and understandings between the Parties.

16.2 No modification or amendment of this Agreement shall be valid unless made in writing and signed by both Parties.

16.3 If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

16.4 The Landlord's failure to enforce any provision of this Agreement shall not constitute a waiver of that provision or the right to enforce it in the future.

16.5 The Tenant acknowledges that she has read and understood all the terms and conditions of this Agreement and has had the opportunity to seek independent legal advice prior to signing. The Tenant agrees that all clauses are fair and reasonable and shall not be subject to challenge on grounds of unconscionability or undue influence.

---
